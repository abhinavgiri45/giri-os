🔍 1. Visual Comparison: Current Giri OS Logo vs Microsoft Windows Logo

An audit of the current official Giri OS logo (giri-os-logo.svg, giri-os-icon.svg) reveals significant visual overlap with Microsoft's registered trademark trade dress (Windows 11, Windows 10, and Microsoft corporate branding).

Microsoft Windows 11 Trademark

  • Geometry: 4 symmetrical squares in a 2×2 Cartesian grid.
  • Aspect Ratio: Exact 1:1 square bounding box.
  • Negative Space: Symmetrical horizontal and vertical dividing cross.
  • Monochrome/Silhouette: Collapses into 4 solid squares.

Current Giri OS Symbol (Flagged)

  • Geometry: Identical 4-pane 2×2 formation; only slight rounding (rx=8-12).
  • Silhouette Identity: In solid monochrome, it is virtually indistinguishable from Windows 11.
  • Small-Scale Test: At 16×16 and 24×24, corner radii vanish; users perceive Windows.
  • Market Perception: Risks being dismissed by Linux communities as a Windows knock-off.
Trademark & Distinctiveness Assessment:

The current 4-pane arrangement does NOT possess sufficient distinctiveness in the operating system category. While the cyan-to-violet palette is differentiated, trademark protection and visual trade dress focus heavily on silhouette, composition, and arrangement of shapes. We explicitly advise transitioning Giri OS to an original monogram or cosmic identity before public release. (Note: This audit is an expert distribution design analysis, not formal legal counsel.)
